EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 1st day of April, 2026, between NORTHWIND SOFTWARE
PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its
registered office at Level 4, Pergola Tech Park, Pune 411014 (hereinafter "the
Company"), and the person named in Schedule A (hereinafter "the Employee").

THIS IS A FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. It was written to exercise this software and is
not anyone's real employment contract. Any resemblance to a real agreement is a
consequence of how formulaic these documents are.

1. APPOINTMENT

1.1 The Company appoints the Employee to the position of Senior Software
Engineer, reporting to the Engineering Manager or such other person as the
Company may designate from time to time.

1.2 This appointment shall be effective from the date of joining recorded in
Schedule A and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Clause 15.

2. PROBATION

2.1 The Employee shall be on probation for a period of six (6) months from the
date of joining, which the Company may extend by a further three (3) months at
its sole discretion.

3. REMUNERATION

3.1 The Employee shall be paid the annual cost to company set out in Schedule A,
payable monthly in arrears after deduction of tax at source and all statutory
contributions.

3.2 Remuneration is confidential to the Employee. The Employee shall not discuss
the particulars of their compensation with other employees of the Company.

4. HOURS AND PLACE OF WORK

4.1 The Employee's normal hours of work shall be forty-five (45) hours per week,
Monday to Friday, and such additional hours as may reasonably be required for
the proper discharge of their duties, for which no separate payment shall be
made.

4.2 The Employee shall devote the whole of their time, attention and skill
during working hours to the business of the Company and shall use their best
efforts to promote the interests of the Company.

5. DUTIES

5.1 The Employee shall perform such duties as are assigned to them and shall
comply with all policies of the Company as amended from time to time, including
the Information Security Policy and the Acceptable Use Policy.

5.2 The Employee shall at all times act in the best interests of the Company and
shall not do anything which brings the Company into disrepute.

6. OUTSIDE WORK AND OTHER ENGAGEMENTS

6.1 During the term of this Agreement the Employee shall not, without the prior
written consent of the Company, engage in any other employment, consultancy,
trade or business, whether or not for remuneration, and whether or not such
activity competes with the business of the Company.

6.2 Consent under Clause 6.1 shall not be unreasonably withheld where the
proposed activity does not interfere with the Employee's duties, does not make
use of the Company's resources, and does not involve a client or competitor of
the Company.

6.3 The Employee shall apply for consent in writing to the Human Resources
department and shall not commence the activity until consent has been received.

7. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

7.1 The Employee shall promptly disclose to the Company in writing any interest,
direct or indirect, which conflicts or may conflict with the interests of the
Company, including any shareholding, directorship, consultancy or family
relationship bearing on the Company's business.

7.2 A failure to disclose an interest under Clause 7.1 shall be treated as
misconduct.

8. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

8.1 The Employee shall not, during employment or at any time after its
termination, disclose to any third party or use for their own benefit any
confidential information of the Company, including source code, architecture,
customer lists, pricing, roadmaps, unpublished financial data and the terms of
the Company's agreements with third parties.

8.2 The obligations in Clause 8.1 shall survive the termination of this
Agreement without limit in time.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 For the purposes of this Clause, "Works" means all inventions, designs,
discoveries, improvements, software, source code, documentation, data, processes
and other work product.

9.2 The Employee agrees that all Works conceived, created or developed by the
Employee during the term of employment, whether or not during working hours,
whether or not using the resources of the Company, and whether or not relating
to the present business of the Company, shall be the sole and exclusive property
of the Company, and the Employee hereby assigns to the Company all right, title
and interest, including copyright and patent, in such Works.

9.3 The Employee shall promptly disclose all such Works to the Company and shall
execute such documents as the Company may require to perfect its title.

9.4 The Employee waives all moral rights in the Works to the extent permitted by
applicable law.

10. COMPANY PROPERTY AND RESOURCES

10.1 Equipment, accounts, licences and credentials provided by the Company are
for the business of the Company and shall not be used for any other purpose.

11. NON-SOLICITATION

11.1 For a period of twelve (12) months after termination the Employee shall not
solicit any client or customer of the Company with whom the Employee dealt in
the twelve (12) months preceding termination, nor entice away any employee of
the Company.

12. DATA PROTECTION

12.1 The Employee consents to the Company processing their personal data for the
purposes of the employment relationship and its statutory obligations.

13. LEAVE

13.1 The Employee shall be entitled to eighteen (18) days of earned leave per
calendar year, accruing monthly, of which no more than twelve (12) days may be
carried forward.

14. NOTICE

14.1 After confirmation, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving
sixty (60) days written notice, or payment of basic salary in lieu thereof at
the Company's discretion.

15. TERMINATION

15.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement without notice in the event of
misconduct, including a breach of Clause 6, Clause 7, Clause 8 or Clause 9.

16. GOVERNING LAW

16.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at
Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

17. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

17.1 This Agreement, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.
